The Solution

CreditBridge — Your Loan
Approval Co-Pilot

Upload your documents. Get a plain-language report in 5 minutes that a banker would take 3 hours to produce manually.

01

Connect Your Financial Data

Upload bank statements + GST returns, or consent via Account Aggregator. Your CIBIL, Udyam status, and GST compliance history are read together — not in silos.

02

AI Reads It Through a Lender's Eyes

Claude processes documents against the live underwriting criteria of 20+ Indian lenders (banks, NBFCs, fintechs). Flags every blocker. Ranks by severity and fix-time.

03

Actionable Report in Plain Hindi/English

"Fix this GST address mismatch first — 15 days, unlocks 8 lenders. Then dispute this CIBIL entry — 45 days. Don't touch the HDFC application until both are done."

04

Ranked Lender Shortlist

3 lenders most likely to approve your current profile, with approval probability and required documentation. No wasted hard pulls on CIBIL.
